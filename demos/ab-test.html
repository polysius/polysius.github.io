A/B test power calculator

From the two-stage recommender. The experiment compared the ranker against recent popularity on hit rate at 10. The defaults below are that experiment.

1. Planning: how many users do you need?

Sample sizes use the standard two-proportion formula. The project's own table uses a slightly different formula (Cohen's h) and differs by under 1%.

2. Analysis: is the observed difference real?

Defaults are the reported hit rates (0.0814 and 0.0933), turned back into user counts by rounding. The project reports p = 0.047 and a lift interval of 0.2% to 31%.

The experiment is simulated on held-out purchases, not run on live traffic. It only covers warm users. The write-up calls the evidence weak, and the two panels show why: the observed lift is smaller than the 22% the test could reliably detect.
